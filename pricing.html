06 / What you are investing in

You’re not just paying for something that looks good.

You’re investing in the thinking, design, engineering and attention that make your digital product work.

  1. 01

    Strategy

    Understanding your business, audience and goals.

    So every later decision has a reason behind it.
  2. 02

    Design

    Creating a distinctive, user-focused experience.

    The visible part, shaped by everything below it.
  3. 03

    Development

    Building reliable functionality and the technology behind it.

    Usually the largest share of the work, and the least visible.
  4. 04

    Testing

    Checking performance, usability, responsiveness and quality.

    On real phones and screens, before you see a launch date.
  5. 05

    Delivery

    Deployment, handover and agreed documentation.

    What “handed over” means is written into your proposal.
  6. 06

    Ongoing support

    Maintenance and further improvements according to the agreed support arrangement.

    Scoped like everything else: clear, not open-ended.

We don’t promise “unlimited” anything. Deliverables, revision rounds and support are agreed in your proposal, so you know exactly what you’re getting and we can deliver exactly that.

07 / Flexibility without compromising honesty

Good solutions don’t always have to be built all at once.

Budgets differ. The honest answer is usually a better order, not a cheaper version.

A
Scenario A

Start with the essentials

For clients who want to launch with the most important features first. A focused first scope avoids complexity you don’t need yet. You launch sooner, learn from real users, and decide what comes next with evidence rather than guesses.

B
Scenario B

Build in phases

For larger ideas that can be developed through planned stages. We prioritise features together and add them over time, each phase with its own scope, timeline and investment, agreed before it starts. You’re never committed to phase three on day one.

C
Scenario C

Plan for the bigger picture

For clients who want a scalable, long-term solution. Architecture, integrations and future expansion are considered from the beginning, so later growth builds on the foundation instead of working around it.

We’ll never recommend adding complexity just to increase a project’s cost. We’ll help you understand what is necessary now and what can wait.

And when a scope doesn’t fit a budget, we’ll say so plainly, and show you the options, rather than promise something we can’t deliver well.
