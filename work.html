Patterns · across the six

Same problem. Different trade.

Every business asks a visitor for something. What it asks first decides whether they answer. Here's how three of our clients ask.

01 Kamini · clinic

“What brings you in?”

Asks the reason. Six plain buttons, no typing. Step 2 carries the answer over (“For: Blood test”), so the call-back starts with the right person.

05 MarketIQ · agency

“Let's grow your business.”

Asks the goal. Service and “what's your goal?” sit next to the contact fields, and a strategist replies within 15 minutes with that context in hand.

04 Clans · solar

“Switch to solar at ₹0.”

Asks the bill. The subsidy is the headline, and the qualifying question is the monthly bill, because that's what sizes the system.

  • Thread A

    A number before a phone number

    Answer the visitor's own question with a figure, and only then ask for anything.

    04.1 Solar savings 02.1 Bags & containers 01.2 Test prices

    Proof you can check

    Not adjectives: records, names and measured results, published where anyone can look.

    04.2 Bills before/after 05.4 Brand wall 05.1 Result per service

    Speak the customer's language

    Sometimes literally Odia, sometimes “heart” instead of “lipid profile”, or containers instead of tonnes.
